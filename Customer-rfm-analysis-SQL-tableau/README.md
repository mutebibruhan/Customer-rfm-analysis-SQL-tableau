## Customer RFM Segmentation Analysis
Segmented customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values using sales_2025 data to identify high-value and at-risk customer groups.

**Tools Used**: SQL, Tableau 
**Period**: 12 months of 2025 sales data

**RFM Logic**:
- **Recency**: Days since last purchase
- **Frequency**: Number of purchases per customer 
- **Monetary**: Total spend per customer

**Customer Segments**:
- Champions: High R, F, M
- Loyal Customers: High F, M
- At Risk: Low R, High F, M
- Lost: Low R, Low F, Low M

**Dashboard Features**:
- Segment distribution and revenue share
- Top segments by average order value
- Filters for time period and product category

**Files**:

- SQL queries for RFM calculation and segmentation
